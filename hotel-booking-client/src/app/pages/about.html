About Aurora Grand

A small hotel run by the same family for more than twenty-five years.

Since 1998

It started with four rooms above a bakery

Nino and Zurab Kiknadze bought the building in 1997, when the roof leaked and the staircase creaked in three places. They fixed the roof, kept the staircase, and opened four rooms the following spring.

Twenty-five years later their daughter runs the front desk, the building has eight rooms, and the staircase still creaks — on purpose.

We are not a chain and we do not want to be. What we can promise is a clean, quiet room, breakfast worth waking up for, and someone at the desk at 3 a.m. if your flight is late.
